# Four-week curriculum

| Week | Lessons | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basics 00–11 | Clean commit history, local remote, safe recovery, project 1 |
| 2 | Intermediate 00–06 | PR diff, meaningful review, squash/rebase/conflict exercises |
| 3 | Intermediate 07–11; Advanced 00–03 | CI review, bisect report, reflog recovery, project 2 |
| 4 | Advanced 04–11 | Release project, security review, interview round |

Daily: **25 min explanation · 45 min commands · 20 min practice/debugging.**

Completion: 36 lessons reviewed, all 12 graded labs passed, three project deliverables documented, and at least 80% of interview prompts explained without notes. GitHub-specific labs may need a repository you control.
